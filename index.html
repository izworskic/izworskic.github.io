This GitHub Pages profile collects the public project side of Chris Izworski's work: small web tools, data-driven local information projects, Great Lakes publishing, Michigan outdoor reports, and writing from Saginaw Bay. Chris is a Bay City, Michigan writer, gardener, and public safety professional. His current public web projects focus on practical local information: river conditions, bird sightings, Great Lakes shipping, gardening, soil, weather, and seasonal Michigan reference pages.
